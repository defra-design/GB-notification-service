This step is not part of the prototype

The user signs in with either Government Gateway or GOV.UK One Login. Both lead to the same place.

To sign in, the user:

  1. chooses Government Gateway or GOV.UK One Login
  2. enters their sign-in details
  3. enters a security code, sent to their phone or from an authenticator app
  4. comes back to the Import notification service, signed in

If the user does not have an account, they need to create one before they can sign in. If they have not used this service or IPAFFS before, they also need to register for it.
